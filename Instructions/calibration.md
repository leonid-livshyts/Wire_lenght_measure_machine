# Calibrating the machine

Calibrate before first use, whenever you switch to a different type of wire, and whenever the lengths the machine measures start to drift.

## Prepare

1. Take a piece of the **same wire** you are going to wind.
2. The longer the piece, the more accurate the result. Use **at least 2 m**, and 5–10 m if you can.
3. Straighten it without stretching it, measure it carefully in millimetres, and cut it to that exact length. Write the number down.

## Calibrate

1. **Start:** press the **measuring encoder** button.
2. **Length:** turn the **user knob** until the display shows the length of your piece in mm.
   - Turn slowly for 1 mm steps, and faster for bigger steps.
   - Click the user knob.
3. **Mode:** turn the knob to choose `Auto` (the motor pulls the wire) or `HAnd` (you pull it yourself), then click.
4. **Pull the wire through:**
   - **`Auto`:** the display shows `tIE End`.
     1. Hold the front end of the wire at the measuring roll.
     2. Push the wire through the roll and tie it to the motor.
     3. Click. The motor starts.
     4. Wait until the **whole piece, including its tail, has passed the roll**, then click to stop the motor.
   - **`HAnd`:** the display shows `0`.
     1. Hold the front end of the wire at the measuring roll.
     2. Pull the whole piece through at a steady pace, keeping it taut, until the **tail has passed the roll**.
     3. Click.
5. **Finish:** the display shows a number, and the new calibration is saved automatically.
   - In `Auto` mode, wait until the motor has fully stopped.
   - Click to finish.

## Check

1. Run the calibration again with the same piece of wire.
2. On step 4, the number on the display should end close to the piece's real length.
3. If it's off by more than you can accept, calibrate once more, pulling more steadily.

## If something goes wrong

- **`Err` at the end:** the roll didn't count anything. Check that the wire actually presses on the roll, then start again.
- **`FLSH Err` at the end:** the calibration couldn't be saved. It works until you switch the machine off. Calibrate again after the next power-on.
- **The wire slipped, or you made a mistake:** just finish the steps and calibrate again. Only the last calibration counts.
